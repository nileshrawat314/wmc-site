From code to live site

7 to 10 working days end to end. Pending vulnerabilities can extend this.

  1. You

    Check security

    Work through the checklist for your stack and confirm compliance before scanning.

  2. Cybersecurity team

    Vulnerability scan

    Manual and automated testing. You receive a report with findings.

  3. You, then cybersecurity team

    Fix and revalidate

    Resolve every finding. The team re-tests before final approval.

  4. You

    Send code to WMC

    Send a Dockerized app or a GitLab link for a static site, with deployment details and a point of contact.

  5. After clearance

    Go live

    DNS and SSL are switched on and the site is public.

Who looks after what

WMC runs the servers. Your team owns the website itself.

WMC takes care of

  • Server space and bandwidth for your site
  • 5 GB disk quota per site, files and database together
  • Backups and uptime monitoring
  • OS and server security patches
  • Domains and subdomains as per Institute guidelines
  • Limited hosting support for SSL and deployment access
  • An IIT Bombay Drupal theme to start from

Your team takes care of

  • Design, content and updates
  • Building and maintaining the application including forms and plugins
  • Application-level security
  • Fixing every reported vulnerability before launch approval
  • Custom integrations such as payment gateways and third-party APIs
  • A named web coordinator who talks to WMC
  • Staying inside the quota unless more is approved
No terminal access
Deployment is handled by WMC, not through backend login.
No external logins
Sites that need login from outside the campus network for content updates are not hosted.
Extra checks can come up
The Computer Centre may raise more requirements after seeing your architecture.
