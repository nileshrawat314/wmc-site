Prepare your submission

Work through three parts in order: check your own site against the security checklist, gather the details WMC needs, then fill in the template.

Security checklists

Work through the list for your stack, then the list for every site. The items are guidance to help you pass the scan the first time. The Computer Centre may raise extra points once it sees how your application is built.

Static HTML Node.js or React Drupal or WordPress Every site

Tick only what applies to your site, and leave the rest blank. Ticks are not saved when you leave the page, so print it (Ctrl+P) if you want a copy.

Static HTML site

For plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript sites synced from GitLab.

All done. Send your compliance confirmation with the request.

Node.js or React app

For dynamic applications delivered as a Docker image.

All done. Send your compliance confirmation with the request.

Drupal or WordPress

For CMS-based sites, including those sent as a CMS dump.

All done. Send your compliance confirmation with the request.

Every site

These apply whatever you are hosting.

All done. Send your compliance confirmation with the request.

Details to send to WMC

Sending everything in one go saves a round of questions. There are three groups: the website, the technical setup and the security basics.

About the website
ItemWhat to provideExample
Department or centreThe full official name of the owning department, academic unit or centre.Department of Chemistry
Purpose of the websiteOne or two sentences on who the site is for and what it does.Public site with admissions information and faculty profiles
URL detailsAny specific addresses or paths the site needs, including old addresses that should redirect./admissions should open the admissions page
Production domainThe domain you want the live site to use. WMC allocates domains and subdomains under Institute guidelines.yourdept.iitb.ac.in
Point of contactYour web coordinator: name, email and phone number.Name, department email, extension
Technical details
ItemWhat to provideExample
Technology stackLanguage, framework and the versions you built with.Node.js 22, Express 4, EJS
Database typeThe database engine and version, or say none.MongoDB 7, MySQL 8, or none
Docker configurationYour Dockerfile, the port the app listens on, how it starts, and the names (never the values) of its environment variables.Port 3000, variables PORT and DB_URL
GitLab repository URLThe official GitLab repository that holds the source code.The link to your project on the institute GitLab
Authentication methodHow visitors or administrators sign in, if they do.Public site only; admin login with JWT and bcrypt-hashed passwords
Security
ItemWhat to provideExample
Security compliance reviewA completed checklist for your stack. Open the checklist page and work through it.Static, Node/React or CMS list finished
HTTPSYour site must work over HTTPS. SSL is configured at go-live.No hard-coded http:// links to your own pages
CAPTCHA on public formsAny form open to the public is protected with a CAPTCHA.Contact form and feedback form
Updated dependenciesLibraries, CMS core and plugins are on current, supported versions.npm audit reviewed; CMS core and modules updated
Official compliance confirmationA formal confirmation from your department or vendor that the security review is done.Sent together with the request
