Service policy

What WMC provides, what it leaves to departments and centres, and how problems are handled. This page summarises the policy in plain language; WMC's official wording applies if there is any difference.

On this page

  1. 1. Introduction
  2. 2. Scope of services
  3. 3. Your responsibilities

1. Introduction

The Webservices Management Cell (WMC) hosts and maintains websites for the Institute's departments and centres. This policy sets out the services WMC offers and what departments and centres are responsible for on their side.

2. Scope of services

WMC will

  • Hosts your website on secure server space
  • Provides bandwidth for smooth access
  • Manages storage with a standard 5 GB quota per site, files and database together; more can be considered case by case
  • Monitors uptime within available resources
  • Takes regular backups
  • Allocates domains and subdomains under Institute guidelines
  • Gives limited technical support for hosting, SSL certificates and deployment access
  • Applies OS and server security patches
  • Deploys your site preferably as a Dockerized app; CMS dumps on a best-effort basis; no terminal or backend access
  • Provides an IIT Bombay Drupal theme to start from

WMC will not

  • Design the site or create content including structure and updates
  • Build or maintain web applications including forms and plugins
  • Integrate custom features such as payment gateways, third-party APIs or special integrations
  • Troubleshoot department-specific issues layout, content accuracy and internal workflows
  • Host sites that need external-network logins for adding or updating content

3. Responsibilities of departments, academic units and centres

  • Content ownership design, updates and accuracy of content
  • Submission format Dockerized images preferred; CMS dumps on a best-effort basis
  • Storage quota stay within the approved limit unless more is officially approved
  • A web coordinator named to talk to WMC
  • Compliance hosted content follows Institute policies and applicable legal guidelines
  • Application-level security including resolving every reported vulnerability before launch approval

4. Escalating issues

Report technical hosting problems to WMC through the official support channel. Design and content problems are handled inside the department or centre.

Please email WMC before visiting in person and wait for a reply. The team is often in the middle of other work, and unannounced visits interrupt it.

5. Policy review

WMC reviews this policy from time to time and updates it as the Institute's operational and security needs change.
