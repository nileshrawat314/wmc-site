Contact WMC

For questions on hosting, compliance, deployment or DNS, write to the Webservices Management Cell. Phone works best during working hours.

What to include in your message

  • Your department or centre and your name as web coordinator
  • The site's address or the domain you are asking for
  • Your stack: static, Node.js or React, Drupal or WordPress
  • What you need: a new request, a status question or a problem on a live site

When you send a request, include your updated source code, checklist confirmations, deployment instructions and compliance details.

Start a hosting request

Please write before you visit

The team is often in the middle of other work. Email first and wait for a reply before coming to the office. Dropping in unannounced to report an issue or ask for something interrupts work that is already under way.

In your email, give your site's address and describe the issue, so the team can look into it before any visit.

Need to change something on your site?

WMC hosts the site. It does not design, build or update it. For changes to content, layout or features, contact your own developer or department.
